# Shared engineering instructions
Applies to ChatGPT, Claude and other coding agents.
- Verify repository visibility before uploading unpublished code, data or internal findings. Never commit credentials, raw creator analytics or personal data. Do not change visibility or grant access without owner approval.
- Use separate branches: chatgpt/experiment-NNN or claude/experiment-NNN. Read current files before editing, preserve earlier experiments, avoid force pushes, request owner review before merging.
- Free-first, minimal tool usage. No paid credits or public deployment. No unauthorized access, noncompliant scraping or artificial engagement.
- Use permitted data, document provenance and terms, predefine hypotheses and metrics, test chronological holdouts and compare against simple baselines. Distinguish synthetic tests from empirical evidence. Report failures and uncertainty.
- Each handoff records changed files, exact tests, observed outcomes, limitations and next steps.
- While public, restrict contributions to public-source research and non-sensitive planning.
